Games

Small games I build for fun, made to be played together with friends or family. They run in the browser on a phone, tablet or laptop.

LIFE! Simulation A board game of an Indonesian life: spin the wheel and ride from primary school to university, work, marriage, a home and retirement. The happiest, most balanced life wins, not just the richest. 2–6 players take turns on one device, and the computer can join in. Inspired by The Game of Life on the PlayStation 1, which I loved playing as a kid. Play now ›
